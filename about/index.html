A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hello,
I’m Shengbo.

A student who likes turning an unfamiliar question into something I can explore, test, and build.

I’m based in Palo Alto and attend Kehillah Jewish High School, with graduation expected in 2027. Before that, I studied at Hamden Hall in Connecticut.

HOW I APPROACH THE WORK

Start with a question.
Stay for the details.

My work moves between applications people interact with and research tools that help investigate a question. Releaf puts sustainability into a daily routine. My Phoenix research asks what a heat map leaves out. The assay planner studies how an experiment might use its measurements more carefully.

I’m interested in the decisions that connect those layers: how data is organized, what an interface makes understandable, and how to tell whether a result supports the claim being made.
