THE QUESTION

A scientific project needs a clear explanation of what it does, how it works, and where it might be useful. For LCG-China’s 2024 project on modifying bacterial cellulose, I led the wiki and entrepreneurship work that connected our team’s research with that wider story.

GoldiGEM 2024 team medal
ExploreAidproposed bandage business plan
01

The project behind the pages

The team’s 2024 entry was titled “Modular Bacterial Cellulose Modification Machine in Use and AI Safety Framework in Synthetic Biology.” Bacterial cellulose is a material grown by microbes; the team worked on a modular way to modify it, and paired the laboratory work with a framework for using AI tools safely in synthetic biology.

02

Giving the research a structure

I directed the team’s website presentation, organizing scientific and business material into accessible explanations of the project, methods, and findings. The wiki gave different parts of the work a shared structure so readers could follow the reasoning as well as the results.

03

Thinking beyond the laboratory

I led development of the ExploreAid business plan for a proposed bacterial-cellulose bandage. The work considered customer needs, competing products, market positioning, production costs, and commercialization risks.

04

A team achievement

LCG-China earned an iGEM Gold Medal in 2024. My contribution focused on wiki development, entrepreneurship, and communicating the team’s scientific work to a wider audience. The laboratory and modelling work belongs to my teammates.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SHOW

The honest edges.

  • ExploreAid was a proposed product and business plan. It was not manufactured, tested clinically or sold.
  • My role was communication and entrepreneurship. I did not lead the laboratory or computational work.
  • A team medal reflects the whole team’s project against iGEM’s criteria, not an individual result.
