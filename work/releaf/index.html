THE QUESTION

Sustainability becomes more approachable when the next step is concrete. I helped build Releaf around the things people already have, the habits they want to change, and a community to share those experiments with.

01

From information to a daily habit

Releaf brings together ways to reuse everyday materials, estimates of carbon emissions, and a dedicated online community. The aim is to give environmental information a useful place in a person’s routine: an idea to try, an action to record, or something to share.

02

The system behind the experience

My work included Django backend features for user accounts, Waterdrop rewards, achievements, and notifications. I also developed supporting AI services for image analysis and retrieving sustainability information. These pieces connect the visible app experience to the accounts, content, and feedback that keep it working.

03

Building with a team

As a cofounder of Rematter Cooperation, I worked across software and the broader project. We raised $2,000 in funding and earned a top-two team placement in the 2025 China High School Social Innovation Competition and Incubation Camp. Releaf reached more than 5,000 downloads.

INSIDE THE ENGINEERING

From a photo to a useful next step.

  1. 01

    A shared request contract

    The supporting AI stack separates the gateway, vision, retrieval, knowledge graph, and response services. Pydantic schemas give them a common language for image inputs, location, citations, warnings, and errors, so mobile requests can move through the pipeline predictably.

  2. 02

    Retrieval with a purpose

    Image analysis feeds material-related searches and reuse suggestions. The knowledge graph connects materials to product ideas and relationships, while retrieved documents provide supporting information. Similarity scores rank candidates; they are not calibrated probabilities that an idea will work.

  3. 03

    Useful under partial failure

    The orchestrator assembles results from several services and reports partial responses explicitly. A local lexical search can stand in when vector retrieval is unavailable. Missing required model checkpoints fail closed, making an unavailable capability visible instead of silently inventing an answer.

These details describe the supporting AI repository, which reports staging readiness. They are separate from the released app’s download figures.
